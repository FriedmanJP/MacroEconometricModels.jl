Bibliography

The complete, canonical bibliography for MacroEconometricModels.jl. Every work cited anywhere in the documentation has exactly one entry here, listed alphabetically by first-author surname and then by year. Individual method pages carry their own topic-scoped reference lists; this page is the master index.

For method-specific references at runtime, refs(model) and refs(io, :method) emit the relevant entries in text, LaTeX, BibTeX, or HTML format (see How to Cite). Journal articles carry a resolving DOI; works published without one carry a JSTOR stable URL, a publisher URL, or an ISBN instead.
